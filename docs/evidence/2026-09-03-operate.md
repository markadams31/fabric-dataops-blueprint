# Step 9 — operate: a second solution, a live data contract, and the heartbeat

**Date** 2026-09-03 · **Runs** build 33708519382 (two-solution green), promote 33708813258/33708821936 (sales + finance to test and prod), prod-schedule 33709113801 (notebook Completed), capacity 33708742401

## What was proven

- **The fiftieth-solution claim, live at n=2.** `solutions/finance/` — a folder with
  one lakehouse — produced workspaces, an identity, environments, a variable and a
  place in the deploy matrix with zero workflow or Terraform edits. Both solutions
  build, deploy, and promote independently (`fail-fast: false` after finance's first
  failure cancelled sales mid-deploy — solutions must fail alone).
- **Shortcuts-as-code as the data contract.** `lh_finance` declares a shortcut into
  sales' `gold/fct_orders` in `shortcuts.metadata.json`; the per-environment target
  IDs live in `parameter.yml` as explicit, versioned contract wiring. Published by
  fabric-cicd in dev, test and prod; read-through verified via OneLake.
- **The scheduled heartbeat works end to end**: resume capacity → run
  `nb_ingest_orders` through the Job Scheduler (Spark, `Completed`) → `dbt build`
  with all 19 tests → notify-on-failure wired. Capacity pause/resume/status is a
  workflow anyone can click.

## The sharp edge worth knowing: workspace Viewer is not OneLake read

The contract grant started as Viewer and failed
(`Failed to publish 'sales_gold_orders'`) — consistently, past propagation, while
the identical call succeeded for an admin. Viewer is a SQL-surface role; OneLake
passthrough needs more, and the least Terraform-manageable grant that works today
is **Contributor** — an over-grant (the consumer *could* write to the producer),
accepted deliberately and tracked on the watch list until finer-grained warehouse
sharing exists.

## Cut deliberately

`reap.yml` (no branched workspaces exist to reap, and the platform identity cannot
see developer-created ones anyway — a real limitation better documented than
half-solved) and `definitions-backup.yml` (git is the definitions backup; a live
export adds machinery without a consumer).
